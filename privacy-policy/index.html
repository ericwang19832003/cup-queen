Privacy Policy

Cup Queen  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Cup Queen ("the App", "we", "us") is a free mobile shell game. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

The following data is stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults:

  • Your high score, best level reached, and total wins
  • Whether you have purchased the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase
  • Whether you have completed your first round (for the tutorial)

If you submit a score to the public leaderboard, your chosen player name and your score are sent to and stored on our servers. This data is displayed publicly on the in-app leaderboard. You choose your player name before each session — you may use a pseudonym. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Cup Queen displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS) — only with your explicit permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

We restrict all ads to general-audience content (G-rated). AdMob's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

If you purchase Remove Ads ($1.99), no advertisements are shown. The AdMob SDK remains initialised but does not load or display any ads.

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14 and later, we ask for your permission before allowing AdMob to use your Advertising Identifier for personalised ads. You can choose "Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track". If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. Your choice can be changed at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Apple Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, the App submits your high score and survival count to two leaderboards. This is handled entirely by Apple. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

5. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's purchase privacy policy applies.

6. Children's Privacy

Cup Queen is rated 12+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention

Game progress (score, level, wins) is stored locally on your device indefinitely until you delete the App. Deleting the App removes all local data. We hold no data on our servers.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. To remove all local game data, simply delete the App from your device. To remove AdMob identifiers, reset your Advertising Identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at: privacy@cupqueengame.com
